Hino Family Autopost

A private scheduling tool for the Hino Family's own social media.

What it is

Hino Family Autopost is a small, private automation operated by the Hino Family. After we finish editing one of our short parenting videos, we approve it in a local management console. A scheduled job then publishes that video to the Hino Family's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok at the date and time we chose, together with our own caption and hashtags.

How TikTok is used

  • Content Posting API (Direct Post) — creates the scheduled post.
  • Scope video.upload — uploads our own edited MP4 (vertical, under 60s) to our own TikTok account.
  • Scope video.publish — publishes that uploaded video as a public post on the same single account (ours).

The integration authenticates exactly one TikTok account — the owner's — via OAuth, and stores only that account's refresh token. It never reads or touches any other user's data. There are no third-party or external end users.

Who can use it

Only the operator (the Hino Family). This is not a public product or service.
