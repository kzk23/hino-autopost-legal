Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-03

This application ("the App") is a private, personal automation operated by the Hino Family. It has no external users.

Data we handle

  • OAuth access/refresh tokens for the operator's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. These are stored only as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are used solely to publish the operator's own videos.
  • The operator's own video files, captions and hashtags.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, read or store any data belonging to other TikTok users.
  • We do not share, sell or transfer any data to third parties.
  • We do not use any analytics or tracking.

Retention & deletion

Tokens are kept only while the automation is in use and can be revoked at any time from the respective platform's settings. Video files are the operator's own and are deleted at the operator's discretion.
